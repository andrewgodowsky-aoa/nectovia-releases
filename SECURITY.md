# Security

## Report a vulnerability

Email hello@diomedes.net with "Security report" in the subject line. That is the address Diomedes Systems LLC publishes for Nectovia.

Tell us:

- what you found and where you found it
- the app version or the page address
- the steps to reproduce it

Send only what shows the problem. Leave out other people's data.

Please keep the details private until we've had time to look into it.

## What this covers

The Nectovia app, and the two sites Diomedes Systems LLC runs: nectovia.diomedes.net and diomedes.net.

Questions about how Nectovia handles your data are answered in [TRUST.md](TRUST.md).
