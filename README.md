# Nectovia

Most AI is an assistant. Nectovia is an engineer on your staff.

Nectovia is made by Diomedes Systems LLC. You give it a problem in your own words. It works from the files in your project folders and the text reports your software already produces, and it shows you what it did.

Nectovia isn't open to the public yet, and not everything on this page is built.

## What the app does today

The Nectovia app is a desktop app for Windows. A project is an ordinary folder on your computer.

- Every conversation runs as Show me first or Go ahead for this task. The mode says what needs your approval.
- You confirm what a job may touch before routine writes can go ahead.
- History records supported changes to project files, with their before and after.
- On eligible work, a second AI reviewer can withhold approval for a change. It hasn't been run against a live AI service yet.
- Answers can carry tables, charts and diagrams, drawn right in the reply.
- From the report files you approve, Nectovia writes a weekly brief that cites where every number came from. No AI call is involved.

The roadmap shows what is available and what is coming: https://nectovia.diomedes.net/roadmap
