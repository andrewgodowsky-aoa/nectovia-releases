# Trust

This page says what the Nectovia app does with your data, and how Nectovia is licensed. It comes from the Trust and security, Privacy and Terms pages on nectovia.diomedes.net, which carry the full text:

- https://nectovia.diomedes.net/security
- https://nectovia.diomedes.net/privacy
- https://nectovia.diomedes.net/terms

Nectovia isn't open to the public yet, and not everything on this page is built.

## Where your files live

A project is an ordinary folder on a computer you control. The app keeps its projects and files on that computer.

## What leaves your computer

When you use a connected cloud AI service, your message and the context for that turn go to that provider. The context can include attached files and earlier messages.

You're responsible for the AI accounts you connect and anything they charge.

## What a job may touch

You confirm what a job may touch before routine writes can go ahead.

Spending money, paying people, messaging anyone outside the business and changing access are set to wait for a person. Today the checks cover built-in file writes and loop-run steps, and no live business connection exists yet.

## What the record shows

For any job, the record shows which AI service and model it used, what was approved, what it was allowed to touch and what it changed.

History keeps the before and after of supported changes to project files. A restore creates a new entry. History doesn't undo every external action. A message that has already been sent can't be taken back, which is why messages outside the business need approval first.

## Checking answers

Every answer shows which AI wrote it, from the AI service's own report. On eligible work, a second AI reviewer can withhold approval for a change. It can never widen what a job may do. It hasn't been run against a live AI service yet.

An answer can be wrong even with a source. Check the row, especially when money, people or access is involved.

## Limits we state plainly

- Nectovia has no security certification today. If your industry needs one, tell us the requirement before you rely on Nectovia for that work.
- Anyone signed in to your computer can reach Nectovia. Run it on a computer only your business uses, with its own sign-in.
- The Windows app is tested on one Windows 11 build and isn't code signed yet.

## How we check our own code

The Nectovia code gets an automated security review every day. A person checks every finding, and fixes are tested before release.

To report a vulnerability, read [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What the waitlist keeps

The waitlist asks for your email address. After you join, an optional survey asks which plan you're interested in, how you'd use Nectovia, where you heard about us and whether you'd like to test early builds.

What you enter is stored in our database with the date and time and a reference number. We may also store a one-way hash of your network address. The address itself is never stored.

Waitlist signups are also copied to a product analytics service, in a project that holds only this list. It receives your email address and your survey answers, and never your network address. We use it to send the waitlist emails.

We use your email to tell you when plans open, and your answers to plan the launch. Every waitlist email has an unsubscribe link. We don't sell your details, and we don't use them for advertising.

The site sets no cookies until you join the waitlist or sign in. Joining sets one cookie for a day, so your survey is saved with your signup and nobody else's.

To have your details deleted, email hello@diomedes.net from the address you used. We'll remove them from the database, the analytics service and our email.
